## What "The Administrator" (the admin) promise
1. **No snooping.** "The Administrator" won't browse your photos or files. "The Administrator" will only access content when you ask "The Administrator" to, to fix a problem you've reported, or if required by law.
2. **No selling, no sharing.** Your data will not be sold, given to advertisers, or used to train AI systems, ever.
3. **Transparency.** "The Administrator" will tell you where your data is stored, how it's backed up, and what changes "The Administrator" make that affect you.
4. **Security effort.** "The Administrator" will keep the system updated, encrypted and backed up to the best of "The Administrator"s ability.
5. **Honesty about failures.** If something goes wrong (data loss, breach, outage), "The Administrator" will tell you promptly and clearly.
6. **Easy exit.** On request, "The Administrator" will export all your data and delete your account.
7. **Notice.** If the service is going to end, "The Administrator" will give at least **[60] days'** notice and help you export your data.

## What "The Administrator" asks of you
1. Use a **strong, unique password** and don't share it.
2. **Keep your own backup** of anything irreplaceable (this is a volunteer-run service, not a guarantee).
3. Respect other people's privacy: **don't upload illegal content** or content that harms others.
4. Be careful when **sharing links** (use passwords and expiry dates for private things).
5. Tell "The Administrator" quickly if you think your account has been compromised.

## Good to know (limits)
- As the administrator "The Administrator" **technically have the ability** to access data on the server. This agreement is a promise, not a technical barrier.
- Backups may keep deleted data for up to **[30] days**.
- Service is **best effort**: there may be downtime.
- "The Administrator" is not a lawyer or a professional hosting company; this is not a commercial service.

## Photos of other people
Photos often include friends, relatives and especially children. Please consider whether they'd be comfortable with a photo being stored and *shared*, and don't create public links for photos of other people without asking them.
