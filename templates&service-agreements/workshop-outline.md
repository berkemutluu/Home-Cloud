# 🧑‍🏫 Workshop: "Who Owns Your Memories?" (45–60 minutes)

**Audience:** family, friends, classmates, a community group · **Group size:** 3–15 · **Tech needed:** a projector or a laptop, printed worksheets

## Learning goals
By the end, participants can:
1. Explain in plain words what a "cloud" is and who runs it
2. Name two ways their personal data may be used by companies
3. Describe what metadata is, with one real example
4. Decide, for themselves, one small step to take

## Plan

| Time | Activity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Hook: "Hands up…"** Who has taken a photo today? Who knows where it's stored? Who's read the terms? | Keep it light; no shaming |
| 0:05 | **Where does a photo go?** Draw the journey of one photo on a whiteboard: phone → company server → ??? | Let participants fill in the "???" |
| 0:15 | **Metadata reveal.** Show a real photo's EXIF data (use *your own* photo or a consenting volunteer's) | Memorable moment: "it knows where I live!" |
| 0:25 | **Footprint audit (pairs).** Use the [worksheet](digital-footprint-audit.md), Parts 1 and 4 | Pairs, no sharing of private answers |
| 0:40 | **Options.** Present three paths: (1) stay but tighten settings, (2) hybrid, (3) self-host. Include honest trade-offs | Show the Mac mini if possible. Make it real |
| 0:50 | **One action.** Everyone writes one thing they'll do this week | Collect anonymous sticky notes |
| 0:55 | **Feedback.** Two questions: "What was clear?" "What was confusing?" | Use this as evidence for your CAS reflection |

## Tips
- **Don't preach.** People have different needs. The goal is informed choice.
- **Don't use participants' real photos** without explicit consent.
- Have a **take-home one-pager** ready.
- Keep **evidence** (photos with consent, feedback forms, attendance numbers) for your CAS portfolio.

## Variations
- 15-minute **lightning version** for a school assembly or club meeting
- **Parents' evening** version focused on children's photos and online safety
- **Seniors' session** focused on scams, passwords and photo backup
