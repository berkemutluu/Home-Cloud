# ❓ FAQ For Users

**Is my data really private?**
It stays on a computer "The Administrator" controls and isn't shared with any company by the software. But "private" has limits: "The Administrator" could technically access it, and all backups need to be secured too. See the [house rules](../templates&service-agreements/house-rules.md).

**Can you see my photos?**
Technically, an administrator can access files on the server. "The Administrator" commit not to look, and you can reduce the risk further by keeping very sensitive items out of the system or be an administrator for your own server!

**What if the Mac mini breaks or is stolen?**
There are backups on a separate drive and an encrypted offsite copy (see [Admin Guide](ADMIN_GUIDE.md#6-backups-the-321-rule)). The disk is also encrypted, so a thief can't simply read it.

**What if you get bored, move away, or graduate?**
Good question. The plan is: [handover plan, e.g. hand the server to a trusted person, or give everyone 60 days' notice and an export of their data]. You are never locked in.

**Do "I need to keep my Google/iCloud account?**
For now, yes. Don't cancel or delete anything until you're confident in the new system and have tested getting a photo back.

**Is it free?**
There is no charge. "The Administrator" runs it as a volunteer service. [Optional: voluntary contribution for electricity/drives.]

**How is this different from just buying an external hard drive?**
A hard drive is private but isolated: no automatic phone backup, no search, no sharing, and a single copy if it fails. This gives you the convenience of a cloud with the ownership of a drive.

**Does this use AI?**
Immich uses machine learning for face grouping and search, but it runs **on the Mac mini**, not on a company's servers.

**How do I leave?**
Tell "The Administrator". "The Administrator" will export your photos (with albums and dates) and files, hand them to you, and then delete your account and data on request.
